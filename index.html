🌍 NER Landslide Early Warning System

AI-Based Early Warning and Landslide Risk Monitoring

⚠️ Alert: This system provides early warning signals for landslide-prone areas in the Northeast Region (NER).

📋 Overview

The NER Landslide Early Warning System is an advanced AI-powered platform designed to monitor and predict landslide risks in the Northeast Region. By analyzing geological, meteorological, and environmental data, our system provides timely alerts to help save lives and protect communities.

✨ Key Features

🤖 AI-Powered Detection

Advanced machine learning algorithms to analyze and predict landslide occurrences with high accuracy.

📊 Real-Time Monitoring

Continuous monitoring of geological parameters and environmental conditions for immediate alerts.

🗺️ Geographic Mapping

Interactive maps showing landslide-prone zones and risk levels across the NER region.

📱 Mobile Alerts

Push notifications and SMS alerts to keep communities informed during critical events.

📈 Historical Data Analysis

Analysis of past landslide events to improve prediction accuracy and identify patterns.

🔔 Multi-Level Alerts

Graduated alert system from low to high risk to guide emergency response measures.

🔧 How It Works

1. Data Collection: Gathers data from multiple sensors and meteorological stations.
2. AI Analysis: Processes data through machine learning models trained on historical landslide events.
3. Risk Assessment: Calculates risk levels based on current conditions and patterns.
4. Alert Generation: Sends automated alerts to authorities and communities based on risk levels.
